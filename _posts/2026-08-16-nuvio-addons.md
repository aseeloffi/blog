## How to Install an Addon

1. Open Nuvio
2. Go to **Settings → Content & Discovery → Addons**
3. Recommended **delete** all default addons 
4. Tap **Add Addon**
5. Paste the `manifest.json` URL into the field
6. Tap **Install**
